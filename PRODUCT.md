# FruitVision product context

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

A Computer Vision learner and portfolio reviewers exploring a local fruit detector.
Upload an image, understand its detections, and inspect the recorded evidence behind
the frozen baseline. Existing README and result reports define the scientific scope.

## Personality and anti-references

Clean, minimal, professional. The user's brief calls for an AI analytics dashboard,
not a cartoon fruit theme or an unstyled default Streamlit page.

## Design principles

- Keep detection controls close to the image and make results easy to compare.
- Distinguish current predictions from saved validation/test experiment results.
- Show empty and error states honestly; never substitute invented numbers.
- Explain approximate measurements and diagnostic evaluation limits in context.
- Keep UI orchestration separate from the existing model and analysis functions.
- Prioritize the uploaded photo and detected classes. Keep settings, raw coordinates,
  and research details behind explicit controls; avoid repeating explanations.
- A model candidate must earn promotion through real comparisons. Never disguise
  an unresolved classification error with a renamed label or filtered screenshot.

## Accessibility and visual direction

Use readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, native labeled controls, responsive
columns, and reduced-motion support. A learner comparing bright photographs on a
laptop in daylight benefits from a neutral light workspace and navy navigation.
Restrained sky-blue accents identify actions and selection. System sans typography,
12px panel corners, and consistent spacing keep the focus on images and evidence.
